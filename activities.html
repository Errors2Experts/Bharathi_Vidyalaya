Activities

We round out academics with yoga, martial arts, dance, music and more — building healthy, well-rounded children.

Learning Beyond Classroom

Discover, Create & Grow

Our activities help children develop confidence, discipline, creativity, curiosity and healthy habits alongside their academic learning.

Mind & Body

Yoga & Wellness

Every week, our youngest learners are guided through simple yoga postures and breathing exercises. It's more than flexibility — yoga helps children build focus, calm their energy before class, and develop body awareness early.

Sessions are kept playful, with teachers turning each pose into a small story so it never feels like a chore.

Focus Flexibility Calm Mid
Strength & Dicipline

Karate & Silambam

Our martial arts sessions — karate and the traditional Tamil art of silambam — teach children discipline, coordination and self-defence in equal measure.

Beyond the physical skill, children learn respect for their instructors and peers, patience while mastering a new move, and the quiet confidence that comes from steady practice.

Discipline Coordination Confidence
Art & Culture

Bharatanatyam & Carnatic Music

Classical dance and Carnatic music classes connect children to their cultural roots while sharpening memory, rhythm and expression. From their very first steps to full stage performances at our Annual Day, students build the courage to perform in front of an audience — a skill that carries far beyond the dance floor.

Culture Rhythm Stage Confidence
Expression

Fancy Dress & Elocution

Annual fancy dress and elocution competitions give even our smallest students a microphone and a stage. Dressing up as their chosen character and speaking a few confident lines teaches public speaking, creativity and the joy of being cheered on — often for the very first time.

Public Speaking Creativity Confidence
Skill-Building

Science Models & Smart Classes

Beyond the arts, children explore science through hands-on model-marketing, stay active with smart class session, and learn through audio-visual smart classes. Giving children a well-balanced week that mixes academics, activity and play.
