Mind & Body

Yoga & Wellness

Yoga and wellness activities provide students with a wonderful opportunity to improve their physical fitness, mental well-being, and inner balance through simple yoga postures, breathing exercises, and mindfulness practices.

From their first breathing exercises in the classroom to guided yoga sessions during school activities, students develop concentration, self-discipline, flexibility, and emotional balance. Every session becomes an opportunity for them to relax their minds, build healthy habits, manage academic stress, and grow into healthy, focused, and confident individuals.

Strength & Dicipline

Karate & Martial Arts

Karate classes provide students with a wonderful opportunity to develop physical fitness, coordination, concentration, and self-defence skills while learning the importance of discipline, respect, and perseverance through structured martial arts training.

From their first training sessions at school to demonstrations during Annual Day and school events, students develop confidence, self-control, patience, and respect for their instructors and peers. Regular practice encourages students to stay active, improve their focus in the classroom, and face challenges with determination, helping them grow into confident, responsible, and disciplined individuals.

Art & Culture

Bharatanatyam & Dance

Bharatanatyam and dance classes provide students with a wonderful opportunity to explore the beauty of Indian classical dance and express themselves through graceful movements, rhythm, and storytelling.

From their first steps in the classroom to captivating performances on stage during our Annual Day and school celebrations, students develop confidence, teamwork, and a deeper appreciation for the art of dance. Every performance becomes an opportunity for them to showcase their talents, celebrate tradition, and grow into confident and expressive individuals.

Traditional Martial Arts

Silambam

Silambam introduces students to the traditional Tamil martial art of staff fighting, helping them develop coordination, balance, agility, and physical fitness. Through guided training, students learn basic staff techniques, controlled movements, and the importance of discipline while connecting with Tamil culture and heritage.

From their first practice sessions at school to demonstrations during Annual Day and school celebrations, students gain confidence, concentration, and respect for this traditional art. Regular practice encourages teamwork, patience, and self-control, helping students stay active while developing a strong sense of cultural pride and personal discipline.

Sports & Fitness

Skating

Skating provides students with a wonderful opportunity to improve balance, coordination, flexibility, and physical fitness while learning to glide with confidence. Through guided practice, children develop essential skating skills, improve their agility, and discover the joy of staying active through sports.

From their first steps on skates to showcasing their skills during school sports events and celebrations, students develop confidence, concentration, patience, and perseverance. Regular skating practice encourages children to overcome challenges, maintain an active lifestyle, and build a spirit of sportsmanship, helping them grow into healthy, confident, and determined individuals.
