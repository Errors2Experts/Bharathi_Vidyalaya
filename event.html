On the Calendar

Events

From science expos to our grand Annual Day, here's what keeps our school year full of energy.

Annual Events

Recent & Recurring Events

38th Annual Day Celebration

Our biggest event of the year — dance, drama, prize distribution and a stage for every student to shine, held at N.G.O. Colony, Dindigul. Parents, teachers and the entire school community come together to celebrate a year of hard work, creativity and growth.

Science & Social Science Edu Expo

Students design and present working models and charts on science and social science themes. The expo encourages curiosity, teamwork and hands-on learning while giving every child a chance to explain their project to visitors and parents.

Fancy Dress & Elocution Competition

Young students dress up as their favourite characters and speak on stage. The event builds creativity, vocabulary and public-speaking confidence — often the first time many children perform in front of an audience.

Independence Day Celebration

Flag hoisting, patriotic songs and cultural performances mark the day with pride and celebration. Students learn the value of freedom and unity while taking part in colourful programmes that honour our nation.

Republic Day Celebration

Students take part in flag hoisting and cultural programmes celebrating the nation. The day includes speeches, songs and a short parade that helps children understand the importance of the Constitution and civic responsibility.

Sports & Yoga Day

A day dedicated to physical activity — races, games and yoga demonstrations for every class. Children discover the joy of movement, teamwork and healthy competition in a fun, supportive atmosphere.

Prize & Certificate Distribution

Recognizing students for academic performance, activity achievements and good conduct throughout the year. Certificates and prizes motivate children and celebrate steady effort as much as top ranks.
